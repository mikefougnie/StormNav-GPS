# StormNav Privacy Policy

**Effective:** November 18, 2026
**App:** StormNav (Android), package `com.stormnav.app`
**Contact:** michaelfougnie@gmail.com

StormNav is a free navigation app that shows weather along your route. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never sells data.

## What StormNav uses, and why

**Your location (precise).** StormNav uses your phone's GPS only while the app is open, to show where you are, give turn-by-turn directions, and check the weather where you are and along your route. StormNav does not use your location in the background.

To provide these features, StormNav sends your location, or places near it, directly from your phone to these services:

| Purpose | Service |
|---|---|
| Driving directions | FOSSGIS OSRM server (routing.openstreetmap.de); backups: openrouteservice (api.openrouteservice.org) and the OSRM demo server (router.project-osrm.org) |
| Searching for places and addresses; street address of your parking spot | Photon by komoot (photon.komoot.io) |
| Street-address lookup | U.S. Census Bureau Geocoder (geocoding.geo.census.gov) |
| Weather forecasts, observations and alerts | U.S. National Weather Service (api.weather.gov) |
| Weather radar images for your area and route | NOAA / National Weather Service (opengeo.ncep.noaa.gov) |
| Nearby gas stations, food, hotels and rest areas | Public OpenStreetMap Overpass servers (overpass-api.de, overpass.private.coffee, maps.mail.ru) |
| Map and satellite images for the area on screen | OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org) and Esri World Imagery (server.arcgisonline.com) |
| Live traffic on the map, traffic-aware first route of each trip, and crash/closure warnings on your route (only while Traffic is on in the Menu) | TomTom Traffic (api.tomtom.com) |

These services receive the request and your phone's IP address, as with any internet request. Each service's own privacy policy applies to what it receives. StormNav has no server of its own that collects your location.

**Things saved only on your phone:** your settings, recent destinations, Home and Work, and your parking spot. They never leave your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

**Voice guidance** uses your phone's built-in text-to-speech engine.

**"Buy me a coffee"** opens PayPal or Venmo in your browser or their app. StormNav receives no payment or personal information; PayPal's and Venmo's privacy policies apply.

**Hazard reports:** the optional shared hazard-report server is not enabled in the published app. If it is enabled in a future version, this policy will be updated first.

## What StormNav does not do

- No accounts or sign-in.
- No advertising or advertising IDs.
- No analytics, crash reporting or tracking SDKs.
- No selling or sharing of personal data for marketing.
- No access to contacts, photos, microphone or files.

## Children

StormNav is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
